**ADP3D Studio** is an all-in-one 3D design and printing software. Describe what you want to make, and it designs, checks and prepares it for your printer. From idea to finished print, it watches over every step for a quality result.

- AI design in English, Marathi or Hindi
- Photo, sketch and scan to 3D
- Smart print check with auto-fix
- Auto-split big models with connectors
- Multi-colour, resin and CNC support

Made in India, for makers everywhere. 🇮🇳
